Mantou Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Mantou ("the app", "we") is a Mandarin-learning app that plays short clips from public YouTube videos with tap-to-translate subtitles. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can delete it. It matches what the app actually does today; we will update it when that changes.

1. Who is responsible

Data controller: Michal Ježek, Philippines.
Contact: mjezek18+mantou@gmail.com

2. What we collect and why

We do not collect your name, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers. We do not show our own ads and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Preferences such as theme, coach hints and the daily reminder are stored only on your device.

3. YouTube

Videos play inside YouTube's embedded player. When a clip loads, YouTube/Google receives the same information as when you watch an embedded video on any website (for example your IP address, device information and which video is played) and may set cookies in the player. YouTube may also show its own ads in the player. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. We do not receive your YouTube account data.

4. Service providers

We share data only with providers who process it for us:

  • Supabase (database, authentication), hosted on Amazon Web Services. It stores the data in the table above.
  • Apple (App Store, in-app purchases, TestFlight crash logs you opt into).
  • RevenueCat (subscription management).
  • Sentry (crash reporting), only if enabled in this build.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Legal basis (EU/UK users)

Performance of our contract with you (running the app), and our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and the content accurate (usage events, crash reports). You can object to usage analytics by deleting your account.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Your account and everything linked to it is kept until you delete it.
  • Delete in the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your account, profile, saved words, history, events and reports immediately. Feedback messages are kept without your user ID.
  • Guest accounts that have not been used for 12 months may be deleted.
  • Crash reports are deleted by our provider after 90 days.
  • If you signed out of a guest account without adding an email, that account cannot be recovered; it is deleted under the 12-month rule.

You can also email us at mjezek18+mantou@gmail.com to request deletion or a copy of your data.

7. Children

The app is intended for learners aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with row-level access control so that only your account can read your data. No method is perfectly secure; contact us if you suspect a problem.

9. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

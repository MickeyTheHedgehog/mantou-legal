Common questions

A clip's Chinese, pinyin or translation is wrong.
Tap ··· under the video → Report clip → pick the reason. We review reported clips every week; two reports pull a clip from everyone's feed immediately.

The video won't play / shows an ad.
Clips are streamed from YouTube. If a video was removed or its owner disabled embedding it will drop out of the feed within a day; report it with "Video won't play" so it goes faster. YouTube may show its own ads before some videos; we don't control that.

I ran out of clips.
The beginner library is small in this first release. The feed will offer slightly harder clips, replay of clips you saved, and review of your saved words until new clips land (every week).

I lost my progress.
Guest accounts live only on the phone that created them. Add an email in Profile → "Add an email to sync" to keep progress across devices and reinstalls.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete account. Everything linked to the account is removed immediately.

Subscriptions and refunds.
If you subscribed, manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple: support.apple.com/HT204084
